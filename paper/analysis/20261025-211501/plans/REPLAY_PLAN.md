# Receiver replay diagnostic plan

Added after the no-thinking screen completed and both low-reasoning A1 pairs
completed with 8/8 correct evaluation choices. Other low-reasoning arms are still
running. This is an adaptively selected exploratory mechanism diagnostic, not an
independent confirmation or a new pair replication.

Use the two completed A1 histories from the low-reasoning scout. For each of their
eight evaluation trials, freeze Alice's committed action, current answer and the
permitted training history. Call Bob once with the actual order and once with an
independently randomized order. Randomize replay-call ordering. Preserve the
original receiver prompt builder and generation settings. Never expose the current
answer, Alice's private input or raw transcripts to Bob. Save exact replay inputs,
choices, usage and a pre-call schedule. No feedback from replays enters later calls.

This uses 32 receiver calls, two isolated $0.20 ledgers and at most the $0.40 reserve.
Combined with the original $0.80 and $2.80 caps, total authorization remains $4.00.
No retries, replacement of failures, or reuse as final-study data. Analyze paired
differences within each original history and then the two history-level differences;
do not attach significance intervals. Include actual-versus-random realized order
counts and retain failures. This estimates immediate decoder reliance conditional
on the two selected histories and frozen sender decisions, not effects on acquisition,
end-to-end adaptation, restraint, or population prevalence.
